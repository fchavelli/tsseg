# Sources and licences

The snapshots contain numerical features and reference columns derived from
the sources below. Their rights are separate from the AGPL-3.0-only licence of
the example code. Sources were retrieved on 3 November 2026 (OpenAlex on
6 November 2026).

| Snapshot | Source and licence | Transformation and reference |
|---|---|---|
| `novel.csv` | Robert Louis Stevenson; Project Gutenberg ebook 43, prepared by David Widger. [Source and terms](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/43). | *Jekyll and Hyde*, main text between the first unindented chapter heading and the Gutenberg end marker. Ten headings removed. Disjoint 300-word windows, final incomplete window omitted; first/third-person pronoun rates. Published chapter positions are references only. Gutenberg lists the text as public domain in the USA. |
| `tides.csv` | NOAA CO-OPS, The Battery, station 8518750. [API](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/). | Verified hourly heights and astronomical predictions, 2012-10-15 through 2012-11-12, UTC, metres, MLLW. Exact complete timestamp join. Residual = observation − prediction. Historical storm dates are independently documented by NOAA/NWS. |
| `dna.csv`, `annotations.csv` | NCBI RefSeq [NC_000932.1, Arabidopsis thaliana chloroplast](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/NC_000932.1). | GC and A/C/G/T fractions in disjoint 400-base windows; final 78 bases omitted from 154,478. Seven rRNA features parsed separately from the frozen GenBank record, with 1-based inclusive coordinates and strands. Only GC is fitted. |
| `climate_titles.csv` | [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org), works API, retrieved 6 November 2026. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). | Yearly counts, 1990–2025, of works whose title matches “climate justice”, “loss and damage”, “just transition” or “climate emergency” (OpenAlex title search, with its stemming), and of works whose title matches *climate*. Rate = 1,000 × count / climate titles; log(1 + rate) is fitted. The query URLs are in `sources.json`; counts change as OpenAlex is updated. Context milestones are defined in `climate_vocabulary.py` and never fitted. |
| (bundled with tsseg) `tsseg/data/mocap/86_01.csv` | Carnegie Mellon University, [CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/), subject 86, trial 01; free for research, acknowledgement requested (see `tsseg/data/README.md`). | First rotation axis of the right and left humerus and femur, 120 frames per second, 4,579 frames. Activity labels as used by Time2State (Wang et al., 2023); activity names from the segmentation ground truth of Barbič et al. (2004). Labels are never fitted. |
| `grid.csv` | National Energy System Operator, Environmental Defense Fund and University of Oxford, [Carbon Intensity API](https://carbonintensity.org.uk/). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). | Every half-hour in January 2024. Three contiguous API requests per endpoint, overlapping boundary records deduplicated; extra preceding interval discarded. Generation percentages are features. Actual carbon intensity is a held-out column from a separate endpoint, partly derived from mix and therefore not an independent label. Met Office storm dates are external context. |
| `speech.csv` | Zohar Jackson and contributors, [Free Spoken Digit Dataset v1.0.10](https://github.com/Jakobovski/free-spoken-digit-dataset/tree/v1.0.10). [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The derived snapshot and audio-derived feature exports retain these terms. | 120 distinct clips: Jackson, Nicolas, Theo; all ten digits and takes 5–8. Six disclosed 20-clip blocks, balanced digits, no inserted silence. Edge silence trimmed at 30 dB below peak. Mean MFCCs 1–12 (coefficient 0 excluded), 24 mel bands, 256-sample FFT, 80-sample hop, 8 kHz. Filename references withheld; three speakers supplied to TICC. |
| `sleep.csv` | Kemp and colleagues, [Sleep-EDF Expanded v1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/), PhysioNet, DOI 10.13026/C2X676. [Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). | PSG SC4001E0, expert hypnogram SC4001EC. Fixed recording-clock 23:00–08:00, 1,080 30-second epochs. Welch spectral features (4-second windows) and recorded EMG envelope; seven fitted features, additional channels retained. Expert R&K stages 3 and 4 merged as deep NREM; no unscored epoch in the selected interval. The supplied record date is a recording coordinate, not a population-level identifier. |

Only the optional preparation step reads the original book, genome, EDF and WAV
files; they stay in an external cache and are not redistributed. The derived
speech features keep the CC BY-SA 4.0 terms of their source.
