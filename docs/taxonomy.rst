Taxonomy
========

.. rst-class:: tx-intro

Every detector in tsseg, placed by **how it searches** for a segmentation
(columns) and by **what makes a segment homogeneous** (rows). Click a method to
open its page, a column or row header to read what it means. The axes follow the taxonomy of the
`TSB-SEG benchmark <https://github.com/fchavelli/TSB-SEG>`_, after the review
of Truong, Oudre and Vayatis (2020), extended to Bayesian search and to state
detection.
